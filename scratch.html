The Perfect Brew Process

Achieving the perfect cup requires precision. Below is a breakdown of my preferred morning ritual, alongside the specific flavor profiles I hunt for.

  1. Weigh and Grind the Beans
    • Use a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio.
    • Grind size: Medium-coarse (resembling sea salt).
  2. Heat the Water
    • Target temperature: 202°F to 205°F.
    • Use filtered water for optimal extraction.
  3. The Pour Over (V60 Method)
    • Bloom the grounds for 45 seconds.
    • Pour in concentric circles avoiding the paper filter.
